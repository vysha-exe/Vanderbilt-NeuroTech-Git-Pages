In this project we **assemble the Ultra Cortex Mark V1** EEG headset, then use the **OpenBCI GUI** to **stream data** from a wearer—great for first-time hardware onboarding and demos.

### What you will do

- Step through assembly with the club’s printed or shared checklist.  
- Seat electrodes / harness for the Ultra Cortex Mark V1 correctly for stable signals.  
- Connect to the board and **stream** in the OpenBCI GUI (verify impedances, watch live traces).
