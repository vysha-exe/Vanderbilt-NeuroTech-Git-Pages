**Musepong** is a **Python** game experiment: you wear a **Muse** headset and use **blinks** (and related beta-band dynamics) to interact with **Pong** on screen—think of adjusting your **beta** signature to move or trigger actions while the ball bounces; blinks may register as events that affect gameplay (exact mechanics vary by build).

### Status

We have not published a repo link yet. When the project lands on GitHub, we will add the **GitHub** button here—until then, ask at a meeting or on [Contact us]({{ site.baseurl }}/contact/) if you want to help port or document it.

### Ideas for contributors

- Clean separation between **LSL / Muse streaming** and **game loop**

- Lightweight UI so it runs reliably on club laptops
